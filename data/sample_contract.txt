LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024, 
between ABC Bank S.A. ("Lender") and John Smith ("Borrower").

1. LOAN AMOUNT
The Lender agrees to provide a loan of $50,000 USD to the Borrower.

2. INTEREST RATE
The loan shall accrue interest at a rate of 12.5% per annum.

3. REPAYMENT TERMS
The Borrower shall repay the loan in 36 monthly installments of $1,670 USD,
beginning February 15, 2024 and ending January 15, 2027.

4. LATE PAYMENT PENALTY
Any payment received more than 5 days after the due date shall incur a 
penalty of 2% of the outstanding balance per month.

5. COLLATERAL
The Borrower agrees to provide their property located at 123 Main Street 
as collateral for this loan.

6. DEFAULT CONDITIONS
The loan shall be considered in default if the Borrower misses 3 consecutive
payments or fails to maintain adequate insurance on the collateral property.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
The Borrower may repay the loan early without penalty after 12 months.
Early repayment before 12 months incurs a fee of 3% of the remaining balance.
